# Operational legal review items

These items require business or counsel decisions and are not customer-facing product claims.

## Before marketplace payments

Confirm Elevare Fit LLC's role as platform or merchant, the marketplace payment model, professional payouts, platform fees, refunds, chargebacks, tax reporting, payment descriptors, and alignment with the Terms of Service before enabling payments.

## Intellectual property

Document ownership of company-created code and content as belonging to Elevare Fit LLC where applicable. Founder- or developer-created IP from before formation may require a written assignment. Third-party software and content remain governed by their own licenses.

## Reviews

Before public reviews launch, implement authenticity controls, a moderation policy, protections against fake or seeded reviews, restrictions on incentivized positive-only reviews, and administrative moderation safeguards.

## Data retention

Adopt a documented retention schedule based on operational, legal, security, fraud-prevention, and dispute needs. Do not promise fixed deletion periods until the company can consistently perform them.

## Other decisions

Counsel should review the unpublished expanded arbitration draft before deployment. The company should separately decide whether professional insurance will become mandatory and whether any additional product-specific language is needed for regulated services.

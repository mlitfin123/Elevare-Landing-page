# ElevareFit SEO Content Opportunities

This document separates future editorial work from the current technical SEO remediation. Topics should be prioritized with Google Search Console query, impression, position, and conversion data before production.

## StageLab cluster

Parent product: `/stagelab/`

- Bodybuilding contest prep app guide: explain what athletes should track and how an app fits beside coaching.
- Weekly contest prep check-in guide: cover consistent photos, bodyweight trends, execution, recovery, and review cadence.
- Bodybuilding prep timeline: organize early prep, main fat-loss phases, final conditioning, peak week, and post-show transition without promising outcomes.
- Peak week tracking guide: focus on recording the existing plan and avoiding unsupported medical or dehydration advice.
- Athlete and coach check-in workflow: explain how shared data can improve communication without replacing professional judgment.

Recommended relationship: link each article to StageLab, relevant Prep Files entries, the contest prep countdown, and the competition timeline generator.

## Logbook cluster

Parent product: `/logbook/`

- Workout tracker guide: explain what to record, how long to keep a program, and how to review progression.
- Macro tracking guide: explain calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and the limits of estimation.
- Bodyweight trend guide: explain daily fluctuation and why weekly trends provide more context.
- Exercise log progression guide: explain sets, reps, load, volume, and when a change is meaningful.
- Building a simple workout and nutrition tracking system: connect daily logging with sustainable review habits.

Recommended relationship: link each article to Logbook, the exercise and workout hubs, and the most relevant calculators.

## Elevare marketplace cluster

Parent product: `/professionals/`

- How to choose a personal trainer: cover goals, scope, communication, pricing, credentials, and consultation questions.
- How to evaluate a nutrition professional: explain the differences among common provider types without giving legal advice or implying equivalent scopes.
- Questions to ask a bodybuilding coach: focus on communication, check-ins, expectations, and competition experience.
- Understanding fitness and wellness credentials: describe how users can review claimed and verified information without presenting Elevare as the credential issuer.
- Online versus in-person coaching: compare service format, accountability, equipment, location, and communication preferences.

Recommended relationship: link each guide to the relevant professional category only when that category has sufficient approved, active, public inventory.

## Calculator and exercise support content

Parent products: `/calculators/`, `/exercises/`, and `/workouts/`

- Use Search Console data to identify calculators with impressions but weak click-through rates before rewriting copy.
- Build methodology explainers only for formulas users regularly question, and link them to the relevant calculator rather than creating duplicate tools.
- Prioritize exercise guides with demonstrated demand, strong database content, and clear links to popular workout templates.
- Avoid publishing thin exercise variants or city/category combinations solely to increase URL count.

## Editorial requirements

- Use a real named author and factual bio when the business is ready to supply them.
- Add first-hand examples only when they are accurate and can be attributed.
- Avoid medical claims, guaranteed outcomes, fabricated statistics, and unsupported credential language.
- Create one strong parent page and intentional supporting articles instead of overlapping posts targeting the same query.
- Measure impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and product CTA engagement before expanding a cluster.

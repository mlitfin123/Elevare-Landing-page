## What Changed This Week?

At this point I'm starting to get really strict with the diet.

There's not much room left for guessing anymore.

Luckily my motivation has been really good.

Training feels good, I'm still pushing hard, and I've been getting all my cardio in.

I've also been switching up the cardio depending on what I feel like doing that day.

Some days I'll run.

Some days I'll do an incline walk.

Other days I'll get on the stair stepper.

Doing the exact same thing every time gets boring really fast, so switching it up makes getting through all five cardio sessions a lot easier for me.

As long as I'm getting the work done, I don't really care which type of cardio I'm doing.

---

## Training Every Day

I also changed my workout program this week.

Some of my workouts were just taking way too long.

I'd get into the gym and end up being there forever trying to fit everything in.

So instead of continuing to cram everything into fewer days, I added another training day.

I'm going to the gym every day now.

That lets me spread everything out a little more and make sure I'm still getting all the work I want without every workout turning into a two-hour event.

Right now I actually like it.

I'm already in the gym most days between training and my five cardio sessions, so adding another training day doesn't really feel like much of an inconvenience.

And with five weeks left, I'm pretty motivated to be there.

StageLab still has me a little behind based on the visual assessment.

Honestly, it's hard for me to tell how much better I look compared to last week.

I think I look a little harder, but week-to-week changes get pretty difficult to see when you're looking at yourself every day.

What isn't hard to see is the weight change.

I dropped 1.4 lbs this week after lowering calories, and StageLab picked up on that.

Based on my current weight trend, I'm also technically on pace to get where I need to be.

Because of that, the recommendation is to leave everything alone for another week instead of immediately making another change.

I'm good with that.

The calorie cut did what it was supposed to do.

Now I want to see if it keeps working.

## My Thoughts

The biggest thing I'm starting to notice now is hunger.

Nothing crazy yet.

I'm not walking around starving all day.

But I'm definitely starting to think about food a little more than I was a few weeks ago.

I knew that was coming.

Next week I'm probably going to start changing around some of the foods I'm eating without actually changing the plan.

Adding more vegetables usually helps me a lot.

They're low enough in calories that I can make the meals physically bigger without adding much to the total.

At this point, anything that makes 2450 calories feel like more food is useful.

Other than that, I still feel pretty good.

Energy hasn't fallen off a cliff.

Training is still strong.

Motivation is still there.

I just know this is around the point where prep can start wearing on me mentally.

Five weeks sounds short, but when you're hungry, training every day, and getting five cardio sessions in every week, it can start feeling a lot longer.

I just need to keep the intensity up and not start letting little things slide because I'm getting tired of prep.
